Emergency First Aid Guide

Critical Life-Saving Procedures • Always Call Emergency Services First

🚨 IN ANY LIFE-THREATENING EMERGENCY: CALL YOUR LOCAL EMERGENCY NUMBER IMMEDIATELY
CALL 108
Person Not Breathing or No Pulse (CPR)
  1. Check responsiveness – shout and gently shake.
  2. Call emergency services (or have someone call).
  3. Open airway – tilt head back, lift chin.
  4. Look, listen, feel for breathing (max 10 seconds).
  5. If not breathing normally → start 30 chest compressions:
    • Place heel of one hand in center of chest
    • Other hand on top, arms straight
    • Push hard and fast (100–120 per minute, ~2 inches deep)
  6. Give 2 rescue breaths (if trained and willing).
    Pinch nose, seal mouth, blow until chest rises.
  7. Repeat 30:2 until help arrives or person starts breathing.
  8. If AED available → turn on and follow voice prompts.
⚠️ Hands-only CPR (compressions only) is effective if you're not trained in rescue breaths.
Choking (Conscious Adult)
  1. Ask: “Are you choking?” If they nod and cannot speak → act fast.
  2. Stand behind them, arms around waist.
  3. Make a fist with one hand, place thumb side just above navel.
  4. Grasp fist with other hand.
  5. Perform quick upward thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) until object is expelled or person becomes unconscious.
If person becomes unconscious → lower to ground and start CPR (chest compressions may dislodge object).
Severe Bleeding
  1. Call emergency services.
  2. Apply firm, direct pressure with clean cloth or hands.
  3. Do NOT remove embedded objects – press around them.
  4. If blood soaks through, add more layers and keep pressing.
  5. If possible, raise wounded limb above heart.
  6. If bleeding not controlled and limb involved → apply tourniquet 2–3 inches above wound (only as last resort).
Heart Attack
  1. Call emergency services immediately.
  2. Have person sit down and rest.
  3. If they have prescribed nitroglycerin → help them take it.
  4. If conscious and not allergic → give 325 mg aspirin to chew (if available).
  5. Stay calm, loosen tight clothing.
  6. Be prepared to perform CPR if they become unconscious and stop breathing.
Stroke (Act F.A.S.T.)
  1. Face – Ask person to smile. Does one side droop?
  2. Arms – Ask to raise both arms. Does one drift downward?
  3. Speech – Ask to repeat a simple phrase. Is speech slurred?
  4. Time – If any symptom → Call emergency services NOW. Note time symptoms started.
Seizure
  1. Stay calm. Most seizures stop on their own.
  2. Clear area of hard or sharp objects.
  3. Do NOT restrain or put anything in mouth.
  4. Cushion head with something soft.
  5. Turn person gently onto side (recovery position) when seizure ends.
  6. Stay with them until fully alert.
  7. Call emergency if seizure >5 minutes, second seizure, injury, or first-time seizure.
Burns (Severe)
  1. Remove from heat source.
  2. Cool burn with cool (not cold) running water for at least 20 minutes.
  3. Remove clothing/jewelry near burn if not stuck.
  4. Cover loosely with clean, non-stick dressing or cling film.
  5. Do NOT use ice, creams, or break blisters.
  6. Seek emergency care for large, deep, facial, or electrical/chemical burns.
