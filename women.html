Women's Issues in Society

Gender Pay Gap

Women continue to earn less than men for comparable work, with the gender pay gap persisting across industries and career levels. This economic disparity affects financial independence and long-term security.

Work-Life Balance

Balancing career aspirations with family responsibilities remains challenging, as women often bear a disproportionate share of household and caregiving duties.

Healthcare Disparities

Women face unique health challenges and often encounter gender bias in medical research, diagnosis, and treatment, leading to gaps in proper healthcare.

Underrepresentation

Women remain underrepresented in leadership positions across politics, business, and other influential sectors, limiting diverse perspectives in decision-making.

Safety Concerns

Women face ongoing safety concerns in public spaces, workplaces, and even homes, affecting their freedom of movement and mental well-being.

Mental Health Stigma

Women experience higher rates of anxiety and depression, yet face stigma when seeking mental health support, particularly in certain cultures and communities.
